Account and sign-in problems

I can't sign in

  • Check that you're using the email address you registered with. Make sure there are no extra spaces.
  • Passwords are case-sensitive. Tap the eye icon to check what you typed.
  • Make sure you're connected to the internet. Signing in needs a connection, even though logging workouts doesn't.
  • Forgot your password? See Resetting your password.

"Your session has expired. Please sign in again."

You'll see this when your sign-in is no longer valid. For example, after you changed or reset your password on another device. Just sign in again. Your data on the device is kept, including workouts that haven't synced yet and a workout in progress. It comes back when you sign in.

"Your account has been suspended."

Accounts can be suspended for breaking the Terms of Service. A suspended account can't sign in or sync. If you think this is a mistake, contact support from your account's email address.

Resetting your password

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password?
  2. Enter your account's email address and tap Send reset link.
  3. Open the email titled "Reset your ZLift password" on your phone and tap the button. If the button doesn't open the app, copy the code from the email into the app instead.
  4. Choose a new password (at least 8 characters) and sign in.
  • The link and code expire after 30 minutes and work only once. If they've expired, request a new one.
  • No email? Check your spam or junk folder and make sure the address is spelled correctly. For privacy reasons, the app shows the same message whether or not an account exists for that address. So if nothing arrives, the account may be registered under a different email.
  • After a reset, you'll get a "Your ZLift password was changed" email, and your other devices are signed out. If you didn't change your password, contact support immediately.
  • Signed in and want a new password? Use Change password under Account in the app.

Deleting your account

In the app

  1. Open the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Your account and its data are deleted from our servers: profile, profile photo, workouts, templates, measurements, notes, friends and bug reports. That account's data, including progress photos, is also removed from the device you delete from. Details, and the small amount of data that may be kept, are in the Privacy Policy.

Data stored on other devices where you used ZLift stays there until you uninstall the app on those devices.

Without the app

If you can't use the app, email TODO: support email address from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my ZLift account". We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete it.

Just want to stop using ZLift without an account? Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Syncing and offline use

  • ZLift saves everything on your phone first, so you can log workouts with no connection. If you're signed in, changes sync automatically the next time you're online.
  • To get your data on a new phone, install ZLift and sign in with the same account. Your profile, workouts, templates, body-weight entries and notes sync from your account.
  • Not synced: progress photos, custom exercises and their photos, and app settings stay on the device where you created them.
  • Workouts recorded without an account are added to the first account that signs in on that device. Sign in on the device you used before creating your account.
  • Missing data? Make sure you're signed in to the right account and connected to the internet, then reopen the app. If it still doesn't appear, report a bug.

Progress photos

Progress photos are stored only on your device. ZLift never uploads them, so we cannot recover them if your phone is lost, reset or the app is uninstalled. They may be included in your own phone backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backup on iOS), depending on your phone's settings. To keep a copy elsewhere, open a photo in the gallery and share it to a place you choose.

Rest-timer notifications

  • Turn them on or off in the app under Account → Rest Timer Notifications.
  • If they still don't appear, allow notifications for ZLift in your phone's system settings. On Android, also check that battery optimisation isn't restricting ZLift.

Friends

  • Friends and leaderboards need an account and an internet connection.
  • To add someone, search for their username or display name and send a friend request. They need to accept it.
  • Incoming friend requests can be accepted or declined on the Manage Friends screen.
  • Removing an existing friend isn't available in the app yet.
  • To report abusive behaviour by another user, contact support with their username.

Reporting a bug

The quickest way is in the app: Account → Support → Report a Bug. It automatically includes the app version, your phone's operating system and version, and the screen you were on. You can attach a screenshot. If you can't sign in, email support instead.

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • what you were trying to do, and what happened instead;
  • the steps to make it happen again, if you can;
  • whether it happens every time or only sometimes;
  • your phone model and its Android or iOS version;
  • the ZLift version, shown at the bottom of the Account tab;
  • whether you were signed in, and whether you were online or offline;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps.

Please don't send your password or a password-reset code. We will never ask for them.
