Track · Progress · Dominate

Your strength training, logged properly.

ZLift is a workout tracker for strength training. Log every set, reuse your routines, look back at your history and keep an eye on your body weight. It works offline, and you can use it without an account.

Built for the gym floor

Everything you need to train, log and review your workouts.

  • Log your workouts

    Record weight and reps for every set, mark warm-up and drop sets, and use the built-in rest timer. Rest-timer notifications are optional.

  • Workout templates

    Save your routines as templates and start a session from them in one tap. You can share templates with your friends.

  • Exercise library

    Hundreds of built-in exercises with illustrations, searchable and filterable. You can also add your own custom exercises.

  • History and progress

    Go back through past workouts and see how your lifts have changed over time.

  • Measures

    Log your body weight in kg or lb, see it on a chart and get estimated calorie and macro targets.

  • Private progress photos

    Add progress photos to your workouts. They stay on your device: ZLift never uploads them.

  • Train with friends

    Add friends, see the templates they share, keep up your streaks and compare lifts on friends-only leaderboards.

  • Works offline

    Your workouts are saved on your phone first. When you sign in, they sync to your account once you're back online.

  • Account optional

    Start training without an account. Create one when you want sync across devices and the social features.

Straightforward by design

How ZLift handles your data and your time.

  • No ads. ZLift doesn't show advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.
  • Progress photos stay on your phone. They're never uploaded to ZLift's servers.
  • Delete your account in the app. You can delete it any time from Account → Delete account.
