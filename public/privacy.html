1. Who we are

ZLift is a workout and strength-training tracker for Android and iOS. It is built and run by an independent developer: an individual, not a company. In this policy, "ZLift", "we" and "us" mean that developer, who is responsible for the information described here.

  • Developer: the individual shown as the developer of ZLift on its Google Play and App Store listings
  • Contact: support@zlift.online

2. Summary

  • You can use ZLift without an account. Your data then stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your profile, workouts, templates, body-weight entries and exercise notes are stored on our servers. That lets them sync and powers the social features.
  • Progress photos are never uploaded. They stay on your device.
  • ZLift has no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and we don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Using ZLift without an account

If you choose "Continue without account", everything you record (workouts, templates, measurements, custom exercises, photos and settings) is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. If you later create an account or sign in on the same device, the data you recorded without an account can be added to that account and synced. This applies only to the first account that signs in on that device, and only if that data was never synced.

3.2 Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address, used to sign in and to send password-reset and security emails.
  • Username and display name.
  • Password. We store it only as a one-way hash (BCrypt). We never store your actual password, and it's never stored on your device.
  • Profile photo (avatar), which is optional. It's resized and its metadata (such as location data) is removed before it's uploaded.
  • Time zone, used to calculate workout streaks correctly.

3.3 Profile information

After you sign up, the app asks for a few profile details. You can't skip this step if you use an account:

  • Date of birth. We store the date and calculate your age from it.
  • Sex (male or female).
  • Height.
  • Body weight, saved as your first weigh-in.
  • Activity level, which you can set in the Measures tab.

ZLift doesn't accept a date of birth that makes you younger than 13 (see Minimum age).

These details are used to estimate your calorie and macro targets. The estimates themselves are calculated on your device and are not stored on our servers. The details above are stored on your device and, with an account, on our servers.

3.4 Body weight and measurements

The body-weight entries you log (date and weight) are stored on your device and, with an account, on our servers.

3.5 Workouts, templates and history

  • Workouts and history: start and end times, the exercises you performed (including their names) and every set (weight, reps, set type such as warm-up or drop set, and timestamps), plus the name of the template you used.
  • Templates: name, icon, the exercises they contain, and whether you've shared them with friends.
  • Exercise notes you write.
  • Custom exercises: the exercise definitions you create are stored only on your device. When you log a custom exercise in a workout, its name and icon reference are saved in that workout and synced with it. Photos you attach to custom exercises stay on your device: only a file-name reference is included, never the image.
  • Personal records are calculated and stored on your device.

3.6 Friends and social features

With an account, you can search for other users, send and accept friend requests, share templates with friends and compare lifts on friends-only leaderboards. To make this work we store your friend connections and requests, and we calculate streaks and lift rankings from your workouts. See section 10 for what other users can see.

3.7 Bug reports

If you send a bug report from the app (Account → Support → Report a Bug), we receive the title and description you write, the app version, your device's operating system name and version, the app screen involved, and an optional screenshot that you choose to attach. Screenshots are resized and their metadata is removed before upload. We don't collect your device model or any device identifiers.

3.8 Technical information

Like any online service, our servers receive your IP address when the app connects. The ZLift server holds IP addresses only in memory to limit repeated sign-in, registration and password-reset attempts. It doesn't write them to its own logs. Our hosting providers may record IP addresses in their own request logs (see section 11). Our server logs contain technical details such as internal IDs, counts and error types. They don't contain your email address, password, tokens or images.

4. Progress photos and other on-device data

Progress photos are stored only on your device. ZLift has no server storage for progress photos, never uploads them, and has no feature that can send them to us. We cannot see, recover or restore them.

  • Progress photos are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and are separated by account, so each account on the device sees only its own photos.
  • You can delete a photo in the app at any time. Photos are also removed when you delete the workout they belong to, delete your account, or uninstall the app.
  • If you share a photo, the app creates a new copy without metadata and passes it to the app you choose in your phone's share sheet. What happens to it after that depends on that app.
  • Custom exercises, custom-exercise photos, personal records and your app settings (theme, units, rest timer, notification preference, tips you've seen) are also stored only on your device.

Device backups

ZLift uses your phone's standard backup settings. If device backups are turned on, your phone's operating system may include ZLift's on-device data, including progress photos, in your own Google (Android) or iCloud/computer (iOS) device backup. Those backups belong to your Google or Apple account, not to ZLift, and we cannot access them. Because progress photos exist only on your device, losing or resetting your phone without a backup also means losing them.

5. Offline use and synchronization

ZLift is designed to work offline. Everything you log is saved on your device first. If you're signed in, the app syncs your account data (profile, workouts, templates, body-weight entries and exercise notes) with our servers whenever a connection is available. That keeps it backed up and available on your other devices. Data on your device is stored separately for each account and for signed-out use.

6. Sign-in and security

  • The app talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes. Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).
  • Changing or resetting your password signs out your other sessions.
  • Other data on your device is protected by your phone's app sandbox and device encryption. ZLift doesn't add its own extra layer of encryption.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

7. Password reset and emails

When you ask to reset your password, we email you a link and a code that expire after 30 minutes. After a reset, we send a short notice that your password was changed. We send only these account and security emails: no newsletters or marketing. Emails are sent through our email provider, Resend (see section 11). Resend receives your email address and the content of the email, including the reset link and code.

8. Notifications and device permissions

  • Notifications: ZLift uses local notifications for the rest timer only, and you can turn them off in Account → Rest Timer Notifications. They're created on your phone. ZLift doesn't use a push-notification service and doesn't register your device for remote notifications.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or pick a progress photo, a custom-exercise photo, a profile photo or a bug-report screenshot. Your phone asks for permission where it requires it.

9. How we use information

We use your information only to:

  • provide the app's features: logging, templates, history, measurements, estimates and social features;
  • create and secure your account, sign you in and let you reset your password;
  • sync your data between your devices;
  • respond to bug reports and support requests;
  • prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service and, where necessary, suspend accounts.

10. Who can see your information

Other ZLift users

  • Any signed-in user can find you by searching for your username or display name, and can see your username, display name and profile photo.
  • Your friends (people whose friend request you accepted, or who accepted yours) can also see the templates you share, your workout streaks and your rankings on lift leaderboards.
  • Other users can't see your email address, profile details, body weight, workout history, notes or progress photos.

ZLift administrators

A small number of ZLift administrators can access account information for support, security and abuse handling. That includes your email, username, display name, profile photo, sex, date of birth, height, time zone, your workout records, your template names and the bug reports you send (including screenshots). Administrators can see how many body-weight entries you have, but not the entries themselves. Administrator actions, such as suspending an account, are recorded in an internal audit log. When you delete your account in the app, that deletion is recorded there too (see Account deletion).

12. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting libraries, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app uses no cookies. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking either.
  • We don't upload your progress photos.
  • ZLift has no social login, so we don't receive data from Google, Apple or Facebook accounts.

14. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account, then confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

  • On our servers, your account and everything linked to it are deleted: profile, profile photo, workouts (including previously deleted ones), templates, shared templates, body-weight entries, exercise notes, friend connections, bug reports and screenshots, and password-reset records.
  • On the device you delete from, ZLift also removes that account's local data, including its progress photos and custom-exercise photos.
  • Data on other devices where you used ZLift stays there until you uninstall the app on those devices. So does data in your own device backups.
  • When you delete your account in the app, we keep a record that the account was deleted: your user ID, username, the deletion action and when it happened, but not your email address. It stays after your account is deleted, for administrative, security and audit purposes, and ZLift has no automatic deletion period for it.
  • Some other records can remain after deletion: copies in database backups, hosting and email provider logs, and administrator audit log entries. See Data retention.

If you can't access the app, you can ask us to delete your account by contacting us at support@zlift.online from the email address registered to your account. More information is on the Support page.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Correct: you can edit your username, display name, profile photo, profile details and measurements in the app.
  • Delete: use in-app account deletion (see section 14).
  • Access or copy: data export isn't available in the app yet. You can request a copy of your data by contacting us.

To make a request, contact us at support@zlift.online. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account owner. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

16. Minimum age

ZLift is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use ZLift. When you set up an account, the app asks for your date of birth and doesn't accept an age under 13. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing where you live, you may only use ZLift with permission from a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 is using ZLift, please contact us.

17. Where data is processed

Our database is hosted by Neon in the AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany) region. Our other service providers (Railway, Resend and Cloudflare) may process data in other countries, which may not be the country you live in.

18. This website

zlift.online is a static website. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising, fonts or scripts from third parties. Cloudflare hosts it and processes standard technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ZLift changes. We'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

20. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@zlift.online. You can also visit the Support page or read our Terms of Service.
